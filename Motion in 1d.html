Kinematics Foundations

1. Distance

Distance is a scalar quantity that represents the total path length traveled by an object, regardless of its direction.

It gives us an idea of how far an object has moved from a reference point and is strictly a non-negative value.

2. Velocity

Velocity is a vector quantity that defines the rate at which an object changes its position over time, encompassing both speed and direction.

The standard formula for average velocity is v = Δx / Δt, where Δx is the displacement and Δt is the change in time.

3. Acceleration & Graphs

Acceleration describes the rate of change of velocity over time. It indicates whether an object is speeding up, slowing down, or changing direction.
