Newton's Third Law

3. Collision & Momentum Exchange

Newton's Third Law states that for every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction. During a collision, Block A exerts a force on Block B, and Block B exerts an identical but oppositely directed force on Block A.

Because these forces act for the exact same duration, the impulses are equal and opposite, leading to the Conservation of Total Momentum.
